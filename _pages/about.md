I'm a first-year student at Johns Hopkins University, majoring in Neuroscience with a minor in Applied Mathematics.

In Dr. Alfredo Kirkwood's lab at the Zanvyl Krieger Mind/Brain Institute, I'm training in whole-cell patch-clamp recording, on a project about how parvalbumin interneurons shape excitation/inhibition balance in visual cortex during the critical period.

I also build software. I co-founded NeuGenesis, a tool that traces every parameter in a scientific model's code to the line that sets it and the source it cites. I built Sangi Tunes, a music and speech learning app for neurodiverse children.

Outside the lab, I play piano for seniors at assisted-living homes in Memphis with Melodies 4 Memphis.
